QUICK ANSWERS

Before you reach out.

A few answers to the questions customers ask most often.

How can I track my shipment?

Enter your shipment or tracking number on our Tracking page to view the latest available delivery status.

What should I do if my shipment is delayed?

Check the latest tracking update first. If you need additional assistance, contact our support team with your shipment number.

Can NEXORA support business shipments?

Yes. Businesses can contact our logistics team to discuss recurring shipments, delivery requirements and tailored logistics solutions.

How quickly will someone respond?

Our team aims to respond as quickly as possible during support hours, with urgent shipment concerns handled through the appropriate channel.
